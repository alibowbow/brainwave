# Cozy Rooms: reference observations and provenance

## Scope and rights

Reference review date: 2026-10-04. Both user-specified JEV galleries were opened in the supported cloud browser, their relevant demo links were followed, and actual rendered pixels were inspected. The observations below concern visible behavior, composition and material cues, not transferred implementations.

- Sonnet gallery: https://alibowbow.github.io/jev/sonnet-html100.html
- Opus gallery: https://alibowbow.github.io/jev/opus-html100.html
- Sonnet source revision linked by the gallery: `d50dc15f93a552cd1cfef8bddb63c3f2e94b7f7d`.
- Opus reference revision identified in the assignment: `86ad33f7928bd6289b84ef9049c39a6d5bbc4fe0`. This review inspected the currently served demos; it did not independently establish that the hosted pages still exactly match that revision.

The assignment records both MiaAI-Lab repositories as having `license=null` with no verified reuse grant. Public viewing does not authorize copying. No HTML, JavaScript, shader, image, illustration, model or audio asset from either collection was imported. Cozy Rooms code, geometry and procedural material maps are independently authored. Any future third-party asset would need its own explicit license/provenance record before inclusion.

## Per-world design transfer

These are adopted or rejected visual principles. They are not claims of copying a specific algorithm, and final implementation details remain documented by the scene source and QA.

| World | Sonnet principle adopted | Opus principle adopted | Explicitly rejected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `relax` / Hearth | S015: flame activity rooted at the wood, layered bright core/orange edge, restrained upward ember response. | O051: separate local light sources, stone/wood/brass reading as different materials; O035: near ceramic rim and contact shadow cues. | Pixel art, outdoor tent/camper, flying log, large flare, identical stones, static triangular illustrated flames, whole-frame exposure pulses. |
| `sleep_prep` / Sleep Room | S092: independent foreground/glass/exterior depth; S049: low-contrast coexistence of colors, reinterpreted as stable moon-blue and dim bedside warmth. | O041: local glass variation rather than a uniform fog overlay; O051: a lamp change that affects its local surroundings while preserving other light sources. | Neon city, giant rainbow bokeh, heavy blur hiding geometry, rain-scene cloning, luminous pastel backdrop, cursor-following glow or conspicuous pulsing. |
| `power_nap` / Nap Terrace | S011: foreground occlusion and parallax across depth layers, implemented in real spatial geometry; S097: irregular branching and clustered leaves. | O078: shared directional motion with smaller local leaf variation and ground dapple; O035: near tactile ceramic/edge/contact treatment. | Picture-box framing, paper-cut layers, miniature diorama, long empty growth intro, seasonal infographic controls, shaking the entire tree or mass shedding. |
| `nature:winter_lodge` / Winter Lodge | S031: depth-dependent flake scale/softness outside the room; S015: restrained fireplace life; S036: differentiated warm wood, metal, cloth and cool window. | O051: indoor warmth and blue snowy distance, tangible frame/sill/lamp construction; O035: substantial cup rim, glazing and contact shadow. | Snow globe, toy village, globe shaking, front-facing flat illustration, repeated uniform fireplace blocks, aurora spectacle, cat interaction, new audio player. |

## Browser route provenance

The reference pages were inspected with the supported cloud-browser CUA route. Screenshots used for comparison were actual page captures, not generated imagery. A few captures in one reference tab timed out; that limitation was not treated as evidence of demo quality. A newly created tab later captured the snow-globe reference successfully.

A pre-existing local QA browser was located read-only at `/workspace/scratch/2bd758a1b983/browser-runtime/chromium`, accompanied by SwiftShader/EGL libraries. Its sibling installed package metadata identifies `@sparticuz/chromium` version `153.0.0`, MIT, repository `git://github.com/Sparticuz/chromium.git`, at `/workspace/scratch/2bd758a1b983/browser-tools/node_modules/@sparticuz/chromium/package.json`. The sibling cafe validation describes successful Chromium 153 / SwiftShader WebGL2 captures, and provides the `SCENE_BROWSER_PATH` mechanism. This reference review did not launch that binary or modify another worker's files. Cozy Rooms' own QA must record the browser it actually launches, exact source/bundle hashes, screenshot dimensions and test results; the discovery above is not a Cozy Rooms test pass.
